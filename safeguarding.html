Safeguarding

Boundaries and clear reporting protect older adults and young participants.

Immediate danger: contact local emergency services, then follow the organization’s reporting process.

Before sessions

  • Use an approved platform, schedule, and contact method.
  • Confirm consent and the presence or availability of the responsible adult or program lead.
  • Keep personal phone numbers, addresses, passwords, and financial information private.
  • Know how to end a call and who to contact.

During sessions

  • Meet only at approved times and in appropriate settings.
  • Do not record without authorization.
  • Do not give medical, legal, or financial advice.
  • Stop if asked or if the person becomes distressed.

If someone shares a concern

  1. Listen calmly and take it seriously.
  2. Do not investigate or promise secrecy.
  3. Use the person’s own words in a factual note.
  4. Report promptly to the designated safeguarding lead.

This page is a framework, not a complete policy. Participating organizations must supply names, contact details, jurisdiction-specific duties, training, supervision, and escalation steps before sessions begin.
