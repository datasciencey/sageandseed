Privacy

Plain-language principles for a static resource website and developing engagement program.

This website

This static site does not use accounts, advertising cookies, or embedded tracking. The contact form opens your own email application. Your email provider’s terms apply.

Participant information

Collect only what is necessary, with informed authorization and a clear purpose. Use anonymized identifiers in working materials where possible. Never place sensitive information in public files, personal devices, or AI tools that have not been explicitly approved.

Sessions

Do not record, screenshot, transcribe, or distribute a session without explicit authorization. Keep links and schedules private. Report accidental disclosure promptly.

AI-assisted preparation

AI features are in development. Personal information must not be submitted without an approved privacy process, consent, human review, appropriate security, and a clear retention policy.

Questions

Contact Sage & Seed. This draft should be reviewed before any program collects personal information.
