Sage & Seed Conversation Essentials

Warmth, respect, patience, and curiosity matter more than a perfect plan.

Do

  • Speak to the person as an adult.
  • Be warm without being patronizing.
  • Use the person’s preferred name.
  • Ask one question at a time.
  • Allow time to respond.
  • Listen for subjects that create visible interest.
  • Build on familiar people, places, music, work, and routines.
  • Use activities as invitations rather than tests.
  • Validate emotions and follow the participant’s lead.
  • Keep the interaction calm and positive.
  • Ask for guidance when something feels beyond your role.

Don’t

  • Do not speak as though the person is a child.
  • Do not test memory or insist on correct answers.
  • Do not argue about facts.
  • Do not overwhelm the participant with choices.
  • Do not speak too quickly or dominate.
  • Do not force an activity.
  • Do not give medical advice.
  • Do not share private information.
  • Do not record or distribute a session without authorization.
  • Do not continue when the participant appears distressed or asks to stop.
If you are concerned: stay calm, do not promise secrecy, note the person’s words accurately, and promptly follow the safeguarding process. In an immediate emergency, contact local emergency services.
