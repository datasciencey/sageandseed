Systematic reviews and evidence syntheses informing the practical guidance.

Methods to Enhance Verbal Communication between Individuals with Alzheimer's Disease and Their Formal and Informal Caregivers

Egan, Bérubé, Racine, Leonard & Rochon · 2010 · International Journal of Alzheimer's Disease

Thirteen experimental studies were reviewed. Personalized memory aids combined with caregiver training emerged as potentially useful, but study limitations weakened certainty.

Communication Training Interventions for Family and Professional Carers of People Living with Dementia

Morris, Horne, McEvoy & Williamson · 2018 · Aging & Mental Health

Training commonly improved knowledge and use of communication strategies. Evidence for effects on the person with dementia and broader psychosocial outcomes was less conclusive.

The Effectiveness and Characteristics of Communication Partner Training Programs for Families of People With Dementia

Folder, Power, Rietdijk, Christensen, Togher & Parker · 2024 · The Gerontologist

Across 30 studies, family programs generally improved communication knowledge and skills; behavior and psychosocial findings were mixed, and study quality varied.

What Are the Communication Guidelines for People with Dementia and Their Carers on the Internet and Are They Evidence Based?

Harris, Webber, Livingston & Beeke · 2025 · Dementia

The review found 164 strategies across 39 websites; only 61% appeared in systematic reviews or formal guidance. It emphasized strengths, emotional safety, cooperation, context, individuality, and broader meaning.

The Experiences and Perceptions of Young People and Older People Living with Dementia in Intergenerational Programmes

Houghton et al. · 2022 · Dementia

This qualitative synthesis described connection, enjoyment, learning, changed perceptions, and reciprocity. Preparation, facilitation, continuity, and suitable activities shaped experience.

Effectiveness of Intergenerational Participation on Residents with Dementia

Lu, Lan, Hsieh & Lan · 2021 · Nursing Open

The review of 13 articles reported improved pleasure and reduced disengagement, but no apparent improvement in quality of life, depression, or overall engagement measures.

This updated review examined professionally delivered therapeutic interventions. Effects and certainty varied by outcome and time point, with limited longer-term evidence.
