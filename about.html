About us

Connection is reciprocal

Older adults are not merely recipients of help, and young people are not substitute caregivers. Each person arrives with curiosity, experience, personality, and something valuable to share.

How the idea grew

Sage & Seed is associated with the Mindfulness and Mental Wellness chapter at Head-Royce School and student community service. Development has been informed by hands-on experience conducting weekly memory-friendly activities with older adults in a senior living community.

The resources here help one young person engage one older adult more comfortably, personally, and meaningfully.

Our working principles

  • See the whole person
  • Prepare, then stay flexible
  • Follow interest, not performance
  • Protect privacy and dignity
  • Be honest about evidence and uncertainty
  • Keep human judgment at the center
