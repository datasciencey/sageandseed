What do I do when…?

They say something that seems incorrect

WHAT MAY BE HAPPENING

The factual detail may matter less than the feeling or meaning.

WHAT TO DO

Respond to emotion; avoid unnecessary correction; ask about the broader experience.

TRY SAYING

“That sounds like it was an important day.”

AVOID

Do not argue about dates, names, or sequence.

WATCH FOR

Watch for rising tension or ease.

IF IT STILL DOES NOT WORK

Move to a sensory or preference question.

Caregivers: report an acute or concerning change through normal clinical or supervisory channels.

This practical resource supports communication and engagement. It is not medical advice or clinical training.
