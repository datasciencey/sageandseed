What do I do when…?

They become emotional

WHAT MAY BE HAPPENING

A memory, loss, joy, or unmet need may be close to the surface.

WHAT TO DO

Stay calm; acknowledge feeling; allow tears; ask what support would help; respect privacy.

TRY SAYING

“This brings up a lot. I’m here with you.”

AVOID

Do not dismiss, interrogate, or force positivity.

WATCH FOR

Watch comfort, escalating distress, and safety.

IF IT STILL DOES NOT WORK

Offer a comforting topic, music, person, or pause; follow safeguarding when needed.

Caregivers: report an acute or concerning change through normal clinical or supervisory channels.

This practical resource supports communication and engagement. It is not medical advice or clinical training.
