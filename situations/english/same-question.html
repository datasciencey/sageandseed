What do I do when…?

They ask the same question repeatedly

WHAT MAY BE HAPPENING

The answer may not stick, or the question may express worry or a need for reassurance.

WHAT TO DO

Answer calmly; use the same simple wording; write or show the answer; respond to emotion.

TRY SAYING

“Your daughter is coming after lunch. You’re safe here while you wait.”

AVOID

Do not point out the repetition.

WATCH FOR

Watch for anxiety beneath the question.

IF IT STILL DOES NOT WORK

Move to a reassuring routine or familiar activity.

Caregivers: report an acute or concerning change through normal clinical or supervisory channels.

This practical resource supports communication and engagement. It is not medical advice or clinical training.
