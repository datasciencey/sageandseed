What do I do when…?

They lose interest

WHAT MAY BE HAPPENING

The topic may not fit, energy may be low, or the activity may feel like work.

WHAT TO DO

Stop the activity; name a familiar interest; invite a choice; follow nonverbal cues.

TRY SAYING

“Shall we leave this and listen to a song?”

AVOID

Do not push for completion.

WATCH FOR

Notice looking away, shorter answers, or renewed energy.

IF IT STILL DOES NOT WORK

Try one personally meaningful item.

Caregivers: report an acute or concerning change through normal clinical or supervisory channels.

This practical resource supports communication and engagement. It is not medical advice or clinical training.
