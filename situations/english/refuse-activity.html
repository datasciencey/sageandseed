What do I do when…?

They do not want the activity

WHAT MAY BE HAPPENING

Refusal is communication and a valid choice.

WHAT TO DO

Accept it; thank them for telling you; offer conversation, another option, or rest.

TRY SAYING

“Of course. We don’t have to do it.”

AVOID

Do not persuade repeatedly or treat refusal as misbehavior.

WATCH FOR

Watch whether pressure is causing distress.

IF IT STILL DOES NOT WORK

Ask what would feel comfortable, or simply be present.

Caregivers: report an acute or concerning change through normal clinical or supervisory channels.

This practical resource supports communication and engagement. It is not medical advice or clinical training.
