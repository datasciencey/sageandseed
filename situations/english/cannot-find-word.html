What do I do when…?

They cannot find the right word

WHAT MAY BE HAPPENING

The idea may be present even when a word is hard to retrieve.

WHAT TO DO

Wait; keep eye contact; offer one possible word only if helpful; attend to gesture and tone.

TRY SAYING

“Take your time. Do you mean the garden?”

AVOID

Do not finish every sentence or pretend to understand.

WATCH FOR

Watch whether help feels welcome.

IF IT STILL DOES NOT WORK

Invite pointing, drawing, or choosing between two images.

Caregivers: report an acute or concerning change through normal clinical or supervisory channels.

This practical resource supports communication and engagement. It is not medical advice or clinical training.
