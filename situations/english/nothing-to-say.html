What do I do when…?

You do not know what to say next

WHAT MAY BE HAPPENING

You do not need to perform. A shared observation can restart connection.

WHAT TO DO

Pause; return to something they enjoyed; share a little about yourself; offer two topics.

TRY SAYING

“Would you rather talk about food or music?”

AVOID

Do not fill every second or turn to memory quizzes.

WATCH FOR

Watch which word, image, or subject creates a spark.

IF IT STILL DOES NOT WORK

Use a Passion Pack or end warmly if the moment is complete.

Caregivers: report an acute or concerning change through normal clinical or supervisory channels.
