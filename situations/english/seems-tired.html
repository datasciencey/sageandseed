What do I do when…?

They seem tired

WHAT MAY BE HAPPENING

Conversation can require effort; time of day, health, or the environment may matter.

WHAT TO DO

Reduce length; slow down; offer rest; end early without disappointment.

TRY SAYING

“You seem ready for a rest. We can finish for today.”

AVOID

Do not interpret fatigue as failure.

WATCH FOR

Watch eyes closing, slower response, discomfort, or sudden change.

IF IT STILL DOES NOT WORK

End warmly; caregivers report unusual fatigue.

Caregivers: report an acute or concerning change through normal clinical or supervisory channels.

This practical resource supports communication and engagement. It is not medical advice or clinical training.
