What do I do when…?

They keep repeating the same story

WHAT MAY BE HAPPENING

The story may carry identity, pleasure, reassurance, or an unmet need.

WHAT TO DO

Receive it as meaningful; listen for a new detail; ask about feeling; connect it to a photo, person, place, or song.

TRY SAYING

“What did you enjoy most about that?”

AVOID

Do not say “You already told me.”

WATCH FOR

Notice warmth, energy, anxiety, or fatigue.

IF IT STILL DOES NOT WORK

Offer a related photo or gently move to another familiar subject.

Caregivers: report an acute or concerning change through normal clinical or supervisory channels.

This practical resource supports communication and engagement. It is not medical advice or clinical training.
