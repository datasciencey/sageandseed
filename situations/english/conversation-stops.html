What do I do when…?

Conversation stops

WHAT MAY BE HAPPENING

A pause is normal; the topic may be finished or too demanding.

WHAT TO DO

Allow silence; summarize a pleasant thread; share something small; offer one familiar prompt.

TRY SAYING

“I liked hearing about your school. Mine had a very noisy bell.”

AVOID

Do not fire off several questions.

WATCH FOR

Watch for renewed eye contact or fatigue.

IF IT STILL DOES NOT WORK

Use a photo, song, object, or a simple choice.

Caregivers: report an acute or concerning change through normal clinical or supervisory channels.

This practical resource supports communication and engagement. It is not medical advice or clinical training.
