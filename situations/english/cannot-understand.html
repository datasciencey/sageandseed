What do I do when…?

You cannot understand what they are saying

WHAT MAY BE HAPPENING

Speech may be unclear, but gesture, key words, expression, and context can carry meaning.

WHAT TO DO

Listen for key words; ask one clarifying choice; use gesture or writing; be honest kindly.

TRY SAYING

“I want to understand. Did you say tea or TV?”

AVOID

Do not pretend or ask them to repeat endlessly.

WATCH FOR

Watch frustration and whether another method helps.

IF IT STILL DOES NOT WORK

Ask about the feeling or show two options.

Caregivers: report an acute or concerning change through normal clinical or supervisory channels.

This practical resource supports communication and engagement. It is not medical advice or clinical training.
