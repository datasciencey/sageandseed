How it works

A relationship, gently supported

Sage & Seed supports private, scheduled, one-to-one online visits between an older adult and a prepared young conversation partner.

1

Prepare

Learn the preferred name, interests, communication style, and anything that helps create comfort. Choose one or two topics and one optional activity.

2

Connect

Meet at a scheduled time in a private, safe, monitored setting. Check sound, light, and camera position before beginning.

3

Engage

Start with ordinary conversation. Follow interest and use a photograph, song, story, or shared activity only when it helps.

4

Build forward

Note what brought smiles, energy, questions, or longer responses. Bring those threads into the next conversation.

What a good session can look like

  • Sustained conversation or spontaneous storytelling
  • The older adult asks a question or makes a choice
  • Smiling, laughter, singing, or humming
  • Interest in an image or familiar subject
  • A wish to keep going or talk again

No scorekeeping

There are no timers, streaks, winners, or rewards for accuracy. A quiet exchange can be meaningful. Activities can always be simplified, paused, changed, or set aside.
