Caregiver Communication Card

Download PDF

PAUSE • CONNECT • SUPPORT

  • Pause. Connect. Support.
  • Approach calmly and gain attention.
  • One idea or instruction at a time.
  • Allow extra response time; use familiar words.
  • Watch expression and body language.
  • Preserve choice and validate emotion.
  • Reduce noise; use familiar routines or objects.
  • Record what works for the next caregiver.
  • Escalate acute or concerning change normally.

This practical resource supports communication and engagement. It is not medical advice or clinical training.
