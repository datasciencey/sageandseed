Young Adult Conversation Card

Download PDF

CONNECT

  • Smile, introduce yourself, and use the preferred name.
  • Start somewhere easy and familiar.
  • One question at a time. Give time.
  • Listen for what lights them up and stay there.
  • Share something about yourself too.
  • Do not quiz, argue, rush, or force.
  • Simplify, offer a choice, or change direction.
  • Connection matters more than getting the answer right.

This practical resource supports communication and engagement. It is not medical advice or clinical training.
