# Sage & Seed Content Enrichment Plan

## Existing project

The current site is a dependency-free static HTML/CSS/JavaScript project. `assets/css/site.css` supplies the shared navy, sage, pale-blue, peach, cream, Merriweather/Inter-inspired visual system. `assets/js/site.js`, `library.js`, and `guides.js` provide navigation, filtering, and data-driven detail pages. Existing resource entry points are `guides.html`, `for-young-adults.html`, `activities.html`, `worksheets.html`, and `research.html`.

## Principal PDFs

Six per language: young-adult starter, caregiver starter, situation collection, activity collection, young-adult card, caregiver card. Total: 18 PDFs under `assets/downloads/`.

## Evidence discovered

Seven local systematic reviews/evidence syntheses cover communication training, verbal communication and memory aids, online communication recommendations, intergenerational programs, and music-based interventions. Full evidence notes are in `EVIDENCE_NOTES.md`.

## Guide structure

1. **Starter booklets:** enough context and practice to begin confidently.
2. **Situation guides:** short “What do I do when…?” response patterns.
3. **Activity and Passion guides:** personalized openings into conversation, never a game catalog.
4. **Always-with-you cards:** condensed prompts for use during interaction.

## Language approach

English is the evidence master. Hindi and Kannada preserve meaning, dignity, safety, boundaries, and evidence strength while adapting examples and retaining familiar English terms such as dementia, caregiver, session, video call, activity, music, and photo where natural.

## Information not invented

No participant outcomes, clinical efficacy claims, certifications, safeguarding contact names, organizational partnerships, or distribution rights for source PDFs are asserted. Local papers remain internal sources and are not public downloads.
